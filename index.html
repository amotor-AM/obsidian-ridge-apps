Support

Email support@obsidianridgelabs.com. A person reads every message and replies within two business days, Monday to Friday, US Pacific time. Please include:

  • the app name and your Jira site, for example yourcompany.atlassian.net
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot if the chart looks wrong (leave out anything confidential)

Billing, licenses, trials and refunds for paid apps are handled by Atlassian. You can manage them in Jira under Apps > Manage apps or at admin.atlassian.com.
