Apps for Atlassian

Small, focused apps for Jira and Confluence Cloud, built on Atlassian Forge. Each one asks for the least access it needs.

Read-only Gantt Chart for Jira

A Gantt chart from any JQL query or saved filter. It only reads, so your issue dates and history never change.

Timesheet Report for Jira

A timesheet by person, epic, project or issue from the worklogs your team already logs in Jira. Short days are flagged, and it never changes a worklog.

Markdown Renderer for Confluence

Write Markdown in a Confluence page and see it rendered, with tables, alerts, highlighted code and Mermaid diagrams. The Markdown is saved in the page.

Support

Email support@obsidianridgelabs.com. A person reads every message and replies within two business days, Monday to Friday, US Pacific time. Please include:

  • the app name and your Jira or Confluence site, for example yourcompany.atlassian.net
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot if something looks wrong (leave out anything confidential)

Billing, licenses, trials and refunds for paid apps are handled by Atlassian. You can manage them in Jira or Confluence under Apps > Manage apps or at admin.atlassian.com.
